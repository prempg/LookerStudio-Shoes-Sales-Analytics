## Key Analytics & Metrics
- **Core KPIs**: Total Sales Amount ($113.33M), Total Cost ($71.23M), Total Quantity Sold (24.05K), Total Transactions (7.83K), and Maximum Single Sale ($42.00K).
- **Geospatial Insights**: Geographic sales concentration mapped across Indian states and territories.
- **Product Hierarchy**: Volume breakdown across Casual, Formal, and Semi-Casual shoe categories.
- **Shoe Segment Share**: Donut visual highlighting footwear models (Single Monk, Sneakers, Loafers, Derby, Brogues, Oxford).
- **State Performance**: State-level revenue, operational costs, and net margins (Top states: UP, West Bengal, Maharashtra).
- **Monthly Revenue Trajectory**: Time-series trend analysis capturing sales velocity from peak months to seasonal dips.
